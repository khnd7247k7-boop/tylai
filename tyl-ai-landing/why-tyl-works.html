About

Built for follow-through.

Most fitness apps give you tools. TYL gives you a system — coaching that adapts, programming that respects your life, and an interface designed to keep you consistent.

Our principles

Science-backed coaching built on eight proven principles.

Consistency over perfection

Small wins compound. TYL is built to help you show up — not chase flawless weeks.

Progressive overload

Your plan advances when you're ready, with clear structure for strength and muscle gain.

Individualized coaching

Goals, equipment, schedule, and experience shape every workout and nutrition target.

Recovery optimization

Training load and habits account for sleep, stress, and how you actually feel day to day.

Accountability systems

Reminders, logging, and check-ins keep momentum when motivation dips.

Expert guidance

Programming draws on exercise science — not random workouts or generic templates.

Sustainable challenge

Hard enough to grow, realistic enough to sustain — especially when life gets loud.

Identity-based change

Lasting transformation happens when training becomes part of who you are, not a phase.

TYL is built around evidence-based coaching principles supported by exercise science research and leading organizations such as the American College of Sports Medicine. Our AI continuously adapts your workouts, nutrition, recovery, and habits to help you make sustainable progress over time.

Transform Your Life LLC is based in Utah. We're in active beta — your feedback shapes what we ship next.
