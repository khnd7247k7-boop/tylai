FAQ

Quick answers about TYL, Premium, and how coaching works.

What is TYL?

A training app with personalized workouts, nutrition tracking, and AI coaching — built to keep you consistent, not overwhelmed.

Who is it for?

Beginners building habits, experienced lifters, and busy people who want structure without complexity.

How much does Premium cost?

$10/month or $99/year. See pricing →

How do I get access after paying?

Use the same email at Stripe checkout and when you sign in to the app. Premium unlocks on TestFlight automatically.

Do I need a gym?

No — plans adapt to a full gym, home setup, or bodyweight only.

How does the AI coach work?

Your coach uses your goals, logs, and profile to suggest workouts and nutrition — not one-size-fits-all templates.
